SANS maps

Privacy policy

Sans browser extension and Sans Maps website · updated November 21, 2026

What the extension does

Sans mutes, skips, or covers parts of shows on Prime Video, Disney+ and Netflix using community-made maps of timestamps. It controls the service's own player. It never records, captures, copies or downloads video or audio.

What the extension sends

  • Which title you're watching: the service name and the title's ID from the page address (for example netflix, 70251669) and its displayed name, so it can fetch that title's map.
  • Subtitle lines with their times: if subtitles are on, the extension sends the subtitle text the service shows and their timestamps, so the server can work out where to mute. Subtitle text is used only while handling that request and is not stored. Only the resulting facts are kept: start time, end time, and category (for example "profanity, 10.4s–11.3s").

Requests contain no name, email, account, cookies or browsing history. The extension has no analytics or tracking and does not read anything on sites other than the three services above.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and server functions that receive these requests.
  • Anthropic (Claude) may receive a title's subtitle lines and timestamps to draft scene timestamps (for example a violent scene). Anthropic processes them under its API terms. Sans stores only the timestamps it returns.

Sans does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Stored on your computer

Your filter choices (mute, skip or cover for each category) are kept in Chrome's extension storage.

Website accounts

Browsing maps needs no account. If you sign in to vote on or correct maps, your email address is stored by Supabase to sign you in, and your votes and edits are linked to your account. Email addresses aren't shown publicly.
