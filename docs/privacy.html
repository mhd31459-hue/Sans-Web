Privacy policy

Sans browser extension and Sans Maps website · updated November 23, 2026

What the extension does

Sans mutes, skips, or covers parts of shows on Prime Video, Disney+ and Netflix using community-made maps of timestamps. It controls the service's own player. It never records, captures, copies or downloads video or audio.

What the extension sends

  • Which title you're watching: the service name and the title's ID from the page address (for example netflix, 70251669) and its displayed name, so it can fetch that title's map.
  • Subtitle lines with their times: if subtitles are on, the extension sends the subtitle text the service shows and their timestamps, so the server can work out where to mute. Subtitle text is used only while handling that request and is not stored. Only the resulting facts are kept: start time, end time, and category (for example "profanity, 10.4s–11.3s").

To stop abuse, the server counts requests per visitor using a one-way hash of the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored, and the counts are deleted after about two days.

  • "Missed one" reports: when you press Option/Alt+M and pick a category and strength, the extension sends the title, the playhead time, the category and the strength. Reports are anonymous.
  • If you link the extension to an account: it sends a device token with requests for your profiles and with your ratings of scenes ("that was strong", "shouldn't be filtered"), so they're tied to your account.

Without a linked account, requests contain no name, email, account, cookies or browsing history. The extension has no analytics or tracking. Besides the three services above, it runs only on this website, to let the website link it to your account.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and server functions that receive these requests.
  • Anthropic (Claude) may receive a title's subtitle lines and timestamps to draft scene timestamps (for example a violent scene). Anthropic processes them under its API terms. Sans stores only the timestamps it returns.

Sans does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Stored on your computer

Your profiles (names such as "Kids", and what each one filters) and which one is active are kept in Chrome's extension storage. If you link an account, the extension also keeps its device token and your email address there, to show which account it's linked to.

Accounts

Browsing maps and using the extension need no account. If you sign in, Supabase stores:

  • your email address, to sign you in;
  • your profiles: their names and filter settings;
  • linked devices: a label such as "Chrome extension on Mac", when each was linked and last used, and a one-way hash of its token (not the token itself). Unlink a device on the Profiles page and its token stops working;
  • your votes, strength ratings and edits on maps.

Email addresses, profiles and devices are visible only to you. Ratings count toward a map's public strength values but aren't shown with your name or email.
